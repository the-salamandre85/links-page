Acknowledgement of Country

As a proud Jingili man and traditional owner, Saul acknowledges that he is creating his works on the lands of the Ngunnawal, Wiradjuri, and Gnambri peoples. He acknowledges the Aboriginal peoples of this country as the traditional owners and custodians of the land, and acknowledges their ongoing, living connection as custodians to the land. He acknowledges the Elders-- past, present and emerging --of his community. He further acknowledges that "ownership" does not denote the full extent of the cultural, spiritual, and historical connections to the land. Aboriginal sovereignty was never ceded. This continent always was and always will be Aboriginal land.
